## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, energetic Neo-Brutalist cafe aesthetic rooted in Kolkata's vibrant culinary culture. Merging the tactile, offbeat charm of Gen Z sticker-and-zine culture with the artisanal coziness of a neighborhood European-style bistro, the UI balances punchy editorial typography with grounded, organic warmth.

### Audience & Mood
- **Target Audience:** College students from Park Street to South Kolkata, remote creative professionals, cafe hoppers, and aesthetic-conscious foodies seeking community, specialty coffee, and intimate bistro dining.
- **Tone:** Spirited, cozy, unapologetically expressive, tactile, and welcoming.
- **Design Movement:** Warm Neo-Brutalism meets Editorial Bistro Chic. It utilizes visible structural contours (1.5px to 2px solid strokes), playful offset hard-edge micro-shadows, sticker-inspired pill chips, and organic parchment surfaces rather than stark, cold black-and-white brutalist planes.

### Core Visual Principles
- **Tactile Warmth:** Surfaces mimic textured art-card paper and sunlit terracotta tilework, avoiding sterile clinical whites.
- **Expressive Hierarchy:** Bold condensed and idiosyncratic display type pairs with highly readable, sturdy grotesque body typography.
- **Playful Modularity:** Components feel like collectable cards, coasters, and menu boards, sporting punchy borders, offset badges, and physical click mechanics.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in natural culinary tones: roasted coffee beans, artisanal matcha, sun-baked Bengal terracotta, and morning sunlight.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#7B3F00` - Roasted Mocha):** The signature brand tone for top-level interactive components, dominant headers, primary buttons, and navigational anchors.
- **Secondary (`#4A6B4E` - Botanical Matcha):** Represents tranquility, fresh ingredients, garden seating, and cafe ambiance. Used for organic accents, secondary buttons, and tag highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#E05A36` - Kolkata Terracotta):** High-energy punch tone used for conversion-critical CTAs (e.g., "Reserve a Table", "Order Online"), promotional banners, and special callout stickers.
- **Neutral (`#2B2119` - Espresso Charcoal):** Grounded deep espresso hue that replaces harsh `#000000`. Used for all structural borders, outlines, iconography, and body typography to retain warmth while maintaining maximum contrast.
- **Backgrounds:** Built entirely on `#FAF5EE` (Alabaster Cream) and `#FFFDF9` (Linen Card) to evoke heavyweight unbleached paper coasters and natural cafe menu cards.

### Dietary & Functional Semantic Colors
- `status-veg`: Deep herbal leaf green (`#2E7D32`) encased in a pill badge with matcha soft background (`#E3EDE2`).
- `status-nonveg`: Rich terracotta crimson (`#C62828`) over terracotta soft (`#F8DFD7`).
- `status-egg`: Ochre amber (`#EF6C00`) with cream backdrop.

## Typography

The typographic voice pairs artistic boldness with crisp, contemporary legibility.

### Type Pairings
- **Headlines (`Epilogue`):** Expressive, geometric, and high-impact. With heavy weights (700 and 800), it commands cafe editorial presence, pulling eyes directly to special roasts, seasonal bakery items, and hero announcements.
- **Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Warm, geometric, and highly readable on modern screens. Its friendly apertures and humanist forms make dense menu descriptions, reviewer testimonials, and bistro stories pleasurable to read.
- **Labels, Buttons, & Badges (`Space Grotesk`):** Technical yet quirky. Adds intentional neo-brutalist structure to navigation links, prices, tags, and reservation timestamps.

### Usage Rules
- All major titles (`headline-lg`, `headline-md`) should use subtle negative letter-spacing for a tight, print-poster aesthetic.
- Category tags and dietary badges (`label-badge`) must be rendered in uppercase to reinforce the physical stamped-ink aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system bounded inside a high-comfort max width of 1200px, creating an intimate, publication-like reading container rather than an overstretched viewport.

### Breakpoints & Grid Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) page margins, 16px gutters. Single column stacked cards with horizontal scroll snaps for reels and featured dessert items.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 24px margins, 20px gutters. 2-column menu grids and balanced media split cards.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, 32px margins, 24px gutters. Asymmetric editorial layouts, dual-pane reservation flows, and photo collage mosaics.

### Rhythmic Density
- Spacing follows an 8pt base grid with 4pt sub-steps for compact badge paddings.
- Micro gaps (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) separate prices and titles.
- Section boundaries utilize generous negative space (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) on cream canvas backdrops to keep the bold borders and stickers feeling deliberate rather than cluttered.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on diffused digital drop shadows, depth is achieved through **Tactile Neo-Brutalist Hard Offsets** paired with solid structural outlines. This imparts a tangible, paper-and-coaster physical feel.

### Shadow Dimensions & Mechanics
- **Structural Outlines:** Every prominent interactive layer, card, button, and sticker uses a solid `1.5px` or `2px` stroke colored with neutral Espresso Charcoal (`#2B2119`).
- **Resting Elevation (Shadow Low):** `box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px #2B2119;` — used on small chips, inputs, and food item cards.
- **Elevated Interactive (Shadow Medium):** `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #2B2119;` — applied to buttons, modal reservation frames, and featured promo cards.
- **Dramatic Pop (Shadow High / Sticker):** `box-shadow: 6px 6px 0px #2B2119;` — applied to floating action badges, popovers, and hovered menu cards.
- **Physical Click State (Active/Pressed):** On active touch or click, buttons physically shift: `transform: translate(2px, 2px);` with `box-shadow: 0px 0px 0px #2B2119;` or reduced 1px offset, directly mimicking a vintage mechanical key press.

## Shapes

The design system pairs structural solidity with inviting organic curves. While pure brutalism uses sharp zero-radius edges, this system employs **Rounded Neo-Brutalism** (`roundedness: 2` / 16px corner radials on large elements) to communicate friendly hospitality.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Base Surfaces & Cards:** `16px` border-radius (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`). Gives food and ambiance photography a soft, collectable polaroid appearance.
- **Modals & Sticky Sheets:** `24px` border-radius (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`).
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Standard Buttons):** `12px` border-radius (`0.75rem`). Balanced ergonomic grip.
- **Badges, Pills & Icon Buttons:** Full rounded pill radius (`9999px`). Stamped badge appearance that contrasts against structured card containers.
- **Sticker Graphics:** Tilted angles (`rotate(-2deg)` to `rotate(3deg)`) combined with pill shapes to simulate artisanal packaging stickers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Terracotta / Sunset):** Background `#E05A36`, text `#FFFDF9`, border `2px solid #2B2119`, shadow `4px 4px 0px #2B2119`. Font `Space Grotesk` 700. On hover: background shifts to `#FF8C42`. On click: `translate(2px, 2px)` with `2px 2px 0px` shadow.
- **Secondary Action (Matcha Green):** Background `#4A6B4E`, text `#FAF5EE`, border `2px solid #2B2119`, shadow `4px 4px 0px #2B2119`. Hover: shifts to `#5D8361`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Action:** Background transparent, text `#2B2119`, border `2px solid #2B2119`, shadow `2px 2px 0px #2B2119`. Hover: background fills with `#FAF5EE`.

### Food & Beverage Menu Cards
- **Container:** Background `#FFFDF9`, border `1.5px solid #2B2119`, border radius `16px`, shadow `4px 4px 0px #2B2119`. Padding `1.25rem`.
- **Media Presentation:** 1:1 or 4:3 aspect ratio imagery set within `12px` rounded sub-frames with a `1.5px solid #2B2119` divider line.
- **Badge Accents:** Positioned at top-left of image: Pill chips (`rounded-full`) with `1.5px solid #2B2119`, font `Space Grotesk` uppercase, e.g., "BESTSELLER", "CHEF'S PICK".
- **Price Tag:** Rendered in `Epilogue` Bold, colored with `#7B3F00`.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- **Veg Indicator:** Compact badge with `status-veg` (`#2E7D32`) text, background `#E3EDE2`, border `1.5px solid #2E7D32`, rounded `9999px`. Features standard FSSAI-style green dot symbol.
- **Non-Veg Indicator:** `status-nonveg` (`#C62828`) text, background `#F8DFD7`, border `1.5px solid #C62828`, rounded `9999px` with red triangle symbol.
- **Category Filter Tabs:** Pill shape, resting state has `#FAF5EE` background, `1.5px solid #2B2119`. Active state shifts to filled `#7B3F00` with cream text and `2px 2px 0px #2B2119` shadow.

### Input Fields & Reservation Form Elements
- **Text Inputs & Selects:** Height `48px`, background `#FFFDF9`, border `2px solid #2B2119`, radius `10px`, shadow `2px 2px 0px #2B2119`. Font `Plus Jakarta Sans` 15px.
- **Focus State:** Border switches to `2px solid #7B3F00`, shadow increases to `4px 4px 0px #7B3F00`, outline none.
- **Date & Party Size Pickers:** Segmented card buttons with tactile toggle feedback. Selected counter highlighted in `#4A6B4E` with cream numerals.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular `20px x 20px`, `2px solid #2B2119` border. Selected state embeds a solid `#7B3F00` circular dot with an 8px diameter.
- **Checkboxes:** Square `20px x 20px` with `4px` corner curve, `2px solid #2B2119`. Active state features `#E05A36` background fill with a clean white stroke checkmark.

### Testimonial & Review Cards
- **Aesthetic:** Designed like vintage handwritten receipt slips and postcards.
- **Container:** Background `#F3EBE0`, border `1.5px solid #2B2119`, radius `14px`, shadow `3px 3px 0px #2B2119`.
- **Google Star Ratings:** Rendered as bold filled terracotta stars (`#E05A36`) accompanied by aggregate rating text in `Space Grotesk`.

### Video Reels Strip
- Vertical aspect ratio `9:16` cards with `16px` border-radius, framed with `2px solid #2B2119`.
- Includes sound/mute pill button overlay in top-right corner with frosted background (`rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.85)`) and `1.5px solid #2B2119` border.
