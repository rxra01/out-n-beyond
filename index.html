Behind The Counter

Born In A South Kolkata Garage ☕

How two college coffee nerds turned a passion for 36-hour sourdough and single-origin beans into Hindustan Park's warmest creative meeting ground.

No Shortcuts. Just Slow Coffee & Soulful Food.

Back in 2021, when South Kolkata’s cafe scene was overflowing with generic syrup lattes and frozen pizzas, we asked a simple question: Why can't Kolkata have a true artisan bistro where the bread is baked that morning and the beans are dialed in with micro-gram scales?

We started roasting small 500g batches of Chikmagalur and Nilgiri Arabica on a vintage drum roaster. We built our 36-hour sourdough starter from scratch (which we still feed twice daily!). Soon, friends started bringing their sketchbooks, laptops, and dogs to our verandah—and Out n Beyond was born.

“We built Out n Beyond not as a cafe where you are rushed out after 30 minutes, but as a neighborhood living room where ideas spark, vinyl records spin, and every cup tells a craft story.”

— Ananya & Rohan, Co-Founders
